# Ashavin V3 — Dark Espresso Editorial Luxury

## Palette
Espresso Black #0A0805, Roasted Espresso #1A120A, Dark Walnut #352415, Copper Champagne #D5A179, Warm Ivory #F2EEEA, Muted Bronze #96765E.

## Editorial approach
Use real supplied Ashavin product photos as faithful product references, with the new synthetic background images clearly classed as artistic composites or conceptual atmospheres. No generated photograph should be described as a factual photograph of the real venue. Layout: asymmetric editorial typography, restrained motion, geometric control system, fine copper dividing lines. Avoid glow, generic gradients, oversized round pills, fake reviews, fake bookings, and fictional brand claims.

## Assets
`v3-cinematic-hero.webp`: coffeehouse atmosphere composited with a cutout of uploaded Ashavin Caramel Macchiato product imagery. `v3-cinematic-matcha.webp`: atmosphere composited with uploaded one-liter Matcha bottle. `v3-lounge.webp` / `v3-atmosphere.webp`: atmospheric illustrative images, not actual venue photos. The promotional product/gallery assets remain as supplied by the user. Client must approve all branding and license uses before publication.
