# Design system — proposed, not extracted official brand identity

Art direction: editorial contemporary / dark espresso + ivory; hero cinemagraphic original SVG (illustration), monogram A original untuk mock-up (bukan logo resmi).

- Ink `#201d19`, cream `#f5f0e8`, ivory `#fffaf3`, sand `#e8dfd1`, accent `#ae8862`, gold `#d7b18a`.
- Display italic: Georgia serif; interface: DM Sans Google Fonts (fallback Manrope/Arial).
- Fluid container max 1380px, gutters clamp(22px,5.5vw,86px) desktop, 23px on small devices.
- Responsive breakpoints 1120, 950, 720, 390px; no utility framework.
- Buttons min-height 51px, keyboard focus visible. Hero media, large serif typography, asymmetric layouts.
- Animation via IntersectionObserver; disabled on prefers-reduced-motion; important content remains visible without JS.

Owner should approve visual identity, actual photos, official logo and typefaces before commercial use.
